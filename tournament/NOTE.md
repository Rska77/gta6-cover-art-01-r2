# VICE SUNSET — Design Tournament Record

Renders are in `tournament/renders/`, finalists in `tournament/finalists/`, the
layout/grade matrix in `tournament/manifest.md`. Contact sheets:
`tournament/sheet-a.jpg`, `tournament/sheet-b.jpg`.

## Field

12 layout x grade variants (`v01`-`v12`) plus 6 judge-note twins (`n01`-`n06`),
all rendered by the app itself at 1080x1080. Judged pairwise on the competition
criteria in order: art first, then any-photo, fast/easy and polish. The last
three are properties of the app, so the cover contest turned on art and finish.

## Round 1

- Top tier: `v01` (Hero & Stack / Vice Sunset), `v07` (Hero & Stack / Neon Night)
  and `v12` (Hero Band / Vice Sunset).
- Last place: `v11` (Collage Four / Monochrome Noir) - monochrome misses the
  sunset-or-neon brief.
- The pier-led variants (`v04`, `v08`, `v10`) read as travel postcards rather
  than game covers.
- Under Neon Night the strip panels of `v03` and `v06` took a clashing teal cast.

## Twins

The note-ON variant won all three pairs (`n02 > n01`, `n04 > n03`, `n06 > n05`),
narrowly: the sticky note is small, well placed, handwritten, and covers neither
the headline nor the hero's face. The shipped covers therefore keep the note ON.

## Finalists

- `f01` = `v01` refined (headline +10%)
- `f02` = `v07` refined
- `f03` = `v12` refined

Each was produced as square, landscape and portrait exports plus an editor
screenshot (`tournament/finalists/f0N.jpg`, `-land.jpg`, `-port.jpg`,
`-editor.jpg`).

## Winner

`f01` (Hero & Stack, Vice Sunset, boulevard) beat `f02` on panel-to-panel grade
coherence. `f03` placed last because the Hero Band hero crop cuts the subject's
face. The entry's screenshots are `f01`'s exports: `screenshots/1-cover-landscape.jpg`,
`screenshots/2-editor.jpg`, `screenshots/3-cover-portrait.jpg`.
